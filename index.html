Rules

  1. Event length: one hour. The CTFd scoreboard locks the moment the timer ends.
  2. Five categories. Each holds nine challenges — three easy, three medium, three hard.
  3. Points: easy = 10, medium = 20, hard = 30. Total possible: 900 points.
  4. Flag format: every flag looks like FOS{...}. Case-sensitive, lowercase, underscores instead of spaces.
  5. Tackle the easy ones first. Build a score, then move up the difficulty ladder. You don't need to solve every challenge to win.
  6. Work alone. Discussion, sharing flags, or screen-peeking is not allowed. Calculators — including a $z$-table on your phone or printed — are fine.
  7. Ties are broken by who reached the score first on CTFd.
  8. This site saves progress. If you accidentally refresh, the flags you've already captured stay revealed.
